Source: ITF Tennis Glossary

Types of shots in tennis:

  • First term- Forehand A groundstroke that is hit with the palm of the racket hand facing the direction of the shot. They are hit after the first bounce.
  • Second term- Backhand A groundstroke that is hit with the back of the racket hand facing forward toward the direction of the shot. They are hit after the first bounce.
  • Third term- Serve The very first shot played in each point. The player tosses the ball in the air and hits it to the opposite side of the court and diagonal box. On each point, if a player misses their first serve, they may hit a second serve.
  • Fourth term- Volley Hit directly out of the air and before the ball bounces, this shot is usually hit close to the net but is legal to be hit from anywhere.

These notes are based on the ITF glossary linked above.
